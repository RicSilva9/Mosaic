# Mosaic

> A social platform for discovering, creating, customizing, and remixing AI video prompts.

**Mosaic** is a community-driven platform designed around the creation and discovery of prompts for AI-generated video.

Rather than functioning only as a prompt library, Mosaic allows creators to publish prompts alongside their generated results, explain how they work, highlight customizable elements, and allow other users to create and publish their own variations.

These variations remain connected to the creations from which they originated, allowing ideas to evolve through the community while preserving their lineage.

## 🧩 The Idea Behind Mosaic

A mosaic is made from individual pieces that, together, create something larger.

Mosaic follows the same principle.

Every creation published by the community becomes a piece that can be discovered, understood, customized, and transformed by others.

A single prompt may evolve into multiple variations created by different people, creating a visible history of how an idea changes over time.
